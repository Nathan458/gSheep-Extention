gSheep — gSheep for Chrome

Bring gSheep to the web with gSheep!

gSheep is a Chrome extension that automatically adds Adrianotiger's gSheep to the webpages you visit, giving your sheep a chance to roam around whilst you browse and make an ordinary webpage an area for a little desktop pet.

Our gSheep extension inserts Adrianotiger's gSheep html on nearly every page you go on to spawn in gSheep. It offers easy customisation, colour options and how many you want.

Features:
Automatically adds gSheep on webpages
Works on all the pages you visit
Easy eSheep customization
Convenient Chrome extension controls
Retains the original eSheep experience

Privacy
gSheep is designed with privacy in mind. It does not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history or personal information to external servers. The extension's webpage access is used to place and run the eSheep content on the pages you visit.
